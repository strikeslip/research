# AI Music Generators

A curated reference of leading AI audio and music generation tools, organized by category.
All URLs verified as publicly accessible.

---

## 🎵 Top-Tier Full Song & Audio Generators

These models create complete, often radio-ready tracks from text prompts.

| Tool | Description | Links |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Suno AI (v4.5)** | High-quality, full-length songs with vocals and diverse genre capabilities. | [Website](https://suno.com) |
| **Udio** | High-fidelity audio, strong vocal coherence, and advanced editing features (inpainting/extension). | [Website](https://www.udio.com) |
| **ElevenLabs Music** | High-realism text-to-music generation built on their voice technology heritage. | [Website](https://elevenlabs.io) |
| **Meta MusicGen** | Open-source model for generating music from text prompts. Part of the AudioCraft library. | [Website](https://audiocraft.metademolab.com) · [GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/audiocraft) |
| **Stable Audio (2.5)** | Latent diffusion model for high-quality, coherent audio tracks up to 3 minutes at 44.1kHz stereo. | [Website](https://stability.ai/stable-audio) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-audio-tools) |
| **Google MusicFX** *(formerly MusicLM)* | Google-researched model generating high-fidelity music from text. Available in AI Test Kitchen. | [Website](https://aitestkitchen.withgoogle.com/tools/music-fx) · [MusicLM Demo](https://google-research.github.io/seanet/musiclm/examples/) |
| **Kimi-Audio** | Versatile open-source "audio-anything" foundation model for understanding, generation, and conversation. | [GitHub](https://github.com/MoonshotAI/Kimi-Audio) · [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-Audio-7B-Instruct) |
| **ACE-Step** | Rapid open-source music foundation model — synthesizes 4 min of music in ~20s on A100. Apache 2.0 licensed. | [GitHub](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step) · [GitHub v1.5](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5) · [Project Page](https://ace-step.github.io) |

## 🎹 Composition & Production-Focused AI

These tools assist in the songwriting and production process, often focusing on structure, MIDI, or stems.

| Tool | Description | Links |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **AIVA** *(Artificial Intelligence Virtual Artist)* | Specializes in cinematic and classical soundtracks with MIDI editing support. | [Website](https://www.aiva.ai) |
| **Soundraw** | Customizable instrumental music; users can tweak structure and instrumentation. | [Website](https://soundraw.io) |
| **Boomy** | Platform for creating and releasing music with a strong focus on ease of use. | [Website](https://boomy.com) |
| **Soundful** | Platform for generating background music and high-quality loops. | [Website](https://soundful.com) |
| **Beatoven.ai** *(Maestro model)* | Original background music creation with a strong licensing focus. | [Website](https://www.beatoven.ai) |
| **Mureka** | Train custom AI models on your own music for style-consistent generation. | [Website](https://www.mureka.ai) |
| **Loudly** | Generates music stems for further editing and production use. | [Website](https://www.loudly.com) |
| **Flow Machines** | Sony CSL project designed to assist in musical composition. | [Website](https://www.flow-machines.com) · [Sony CSL](https://csl.sony.fr/projects/flow-machines/) |
| **BandLab SongStarter** | Generates musical ideas to kickstart a new project. | [Website](https://www.bandlab.com/songstarter) |

## 🎤 AI Singing & Voice Synthesis Models

These tools target voice generation and cloning, designed to replace or enhance human vocalists.

| Tool | Description | Links |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Synthesizer V Studio Pro** | Leading AI-driven voice synthesis tool with vocal morphing capabilities. | [Website](https://dreamtonics.com/synthesizerv) |
| **ACE Studio 2.0** | Professional AI music studio specializing in high-quality AI vocals. | [Website](https://ace-studio.timedomain.cn) |
| **Vocaloid** *(Yamaha)* | Long-standing voice synthesizer platform with AI enhancements. | [Website](https://www.vocaloid.com) |
| **F5-TTS** | Next-gen open-source voice cloning model using flow matching. | [GitHub](https://github.com/SWivid/F5-TTS) |
| **Fish Speech** | Open-source next-gen voice cloning and TTS model. | [GitHub](https://github.com/fishaudio/fish-speech) · [Website](https://speech.fish.audio) |
| **Kits AI** | Platform for training and deploying custom voice models for music. | [Website](https://kits.ai) |
| **Emvoice** | AI singing synthesizer plugin for DAW integration. | [Website](https://emvoice.com) |

> **Notes:**
> - Commercial platforms (Suno, Udio, AIVA, Boomy, etc.) have no public code repositories — website links are the primary access point.
> - Open-source models with GitHub repos can be run locally or fine-tuned on custom datasets.
> - ACE-Step v1.5 is noted as outperforming most commercial alternatives on consumer hardware (under 4GB VRAM) as of early 2026.
> - All GitHub repositories listed are public and open-source under permissive licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0).
