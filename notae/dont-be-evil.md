# Google AI Ecosystem: A Comprehensive Reference

This repository serves as a curated list of essential links for Google's AI ecosystem, covering everything from consumer chatbots and research tools to developer APIs and world models.

## 🚀 Core AI Platforms & Chatbots

- **[Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/prompts/new_chat)** - The fastest way to build with Gemini models. Ideal for prototyping and prompt engineering.
- **[Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/app)** - Google's flagship consumer-facing conversational AI (formerly Bard).
- **[Google One AI Premium](https://one.google.com/ai)** - Subscription plans for advanced AI features across Google Workspace and Gemini.

## 🧪 Research & Specialized Tools

- **[Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/)** - The official home of Google's world-leading AI research team.
- **[Google NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google/?hl=en-US)** - An AI-powered research and note-taking assistant that uses your own data as a source of truth.
- **[Genie 3](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/)** - Exploring the new frontier for world models and interactive environments.

## 🎬 Veo 3.1 & Creative Tools (Video Generation)

Google's state-of-the-art video generation model and creative workflows.

- **[Veo 3.1 & Gemini API Update](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/introducing-veo-3-1-and-new-creative-capabilities-in-the-gemini-api/)** - Official announcement of new creative capabilities.
- **[Ultimate Prompting Guide for Veo 3.1](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/ultimate-prompting-guide-for-veo-3-1)** - Best practices for generating high-quality video content.
- **[Veo 3.1: Complete Guide with Examples](https://www.datacamp.com/tutorial/veo-3-1-complete-guide-with-examples)** - A DataCamp tutorial on utilizing the model.
- **[VEO - FLOW](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow)** - Experimental tool for cinematic control and motion in video generation.

## 🛠 Developer Resources & Open Source

- **[Google on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/google)** - Official repository for Google's open-weights models and datasets (e.g., Gemma).
- **[Google MCP Servers](https://github.com/google/mcp)** - Official implementation of the Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers by Google.
- **[Google Labs: Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow)** - Creative experimentation interface for advanced AI workflows.
