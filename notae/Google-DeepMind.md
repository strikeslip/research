## DiscoRL (Discovering Reinforcement Learning)
**DiscoRL** is a Google DeepMind project for **automating the discovery of reinforcement learning algorithms** (AI architecture design). "RL" stands for Reinforcement Learning, **not** music-related (e.g., not "Rhythm and Lyrics").

- [google-deepmind.github.io/disco_rl/](https://google-deepmind.github.io/disco_rl/)
- [GitHub: google-deepmind/disco_rl](https://github.com/google-deepmind/disco_rl)
- Nature paper: [Discovering state-of-the-art reinforcement learning algorithms](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09761-x)
- PDF: [researchgate.net/publication/396810029](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/396810029_Discovering_state-of-the-art_reinforcement_learning_algorithms)
- Reddit discussion: [reddit.com/r/accelerate/comments/1pb48nc](https://www.reddit.com/r/accelerate/comments/1pb48nc/google_deepmind_introduces_discorl_automating_the/)
- Rewire.it: [DiscoRL: When Algorithms Learn to Design Algorithms](https://rewire.it/blog/disco-rl-when-algorithms-learn-to-design-algorithms/)
- Chinese review: [xenospectrum.com/the-shock-of-the-birth-of-discorl](https://xenospectrum.com/the-shock-of-the-birth-of-discorl-an-algorithm-that-surpasses-humans/)

**Why Disco57 (or DiscoRL benchmarks on 57 Atari games)?**  
The "57" refers to the classic **Atari57** benchmark suite (57 Atari 2600 games from the Arcade Learning Environment), a standard for evaluating RL agents' generality. DeepMind has historically used it (e.g., Agent57 outperformed humans on all 57 games).

## DeepMind Music AI Projects Overview
DeepMind's music projects (separate from DiscoRL) include:

- **MusicRL**: Builds on MusicLM with RLHF (reinforcement learning from human preferences) for better audio quality and text adherence.  
  [google-research.github.io/seanet/musiclm/rlhf/](https://google-research.github.io/seanet/musiclm/rlhf/)  
  arXiv: [arxiv.org/abs/2402.04229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04229)

- **Lyria**: Advanced music generation (high-quality instrumentals/vocals, style control). Powers tools like Dream Track, MusicFX DJ, Music AI Sandbox.

- **Music AI Sandbox & MusicFX**: Experimental tools for generating ideas, vocals, instrumentals.  
  [deepmind.google/blog/music-ai-sandbox-now-with-new-features-and-broader-access/](https://deepmind.google/blog/music-ai-sandbox-now-with-new-features-and-broader-access/)  
  [deepmind.google/blog/new-generative-ai-tools-open-the-doors-of-music-creation/](https://deepmind.google/blog/new-generative-ai-tools-open-the-doors-of-music-creation/)

- **V2A**: Video-to-audio (soundscapes + music/effects from video + prompts).

**DiscoRL vs. MusicRL**:
- DiscoRL: General RL algorithm discovery / evolution (compute + environment diversity scaling).
- MusicRL: Applies RL (e.g., RLHF) specifically to align music generation with user/listener preferences.
