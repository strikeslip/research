
GEOMAGNETISM NOTES

Earth's magnetic field, generated by its molten core, may subtly interact with seismic activity and volcanic phenomena. Small magnetic anomalies sometimes precede earthquakes, possibly due to stressed rocks or fluid currents. Geomagnetic storms from solar activity might slightly increase quake risk by stressing faults. Volcanic lightning during eruptions can also create brief magnetic disturbances. Though these effects are weak and challenging to measure, geoscientists are actively researching these phenomena using advanced tools like magnetometer networks and satellite data to unravel their potential for improving hazard forecasting.
